There are times when everything may feel difficult.

What Is Depression?

Depression is more than feeling sad for a short period. It can involve ongoing emotional and psychological difficulties that affect different areas of everyday life.

Depression can affect people differently, and not everyone experiences the same symptoms.

Common Experiences

Some people may experience:

  • Persistent low mood
  • Loss of interest in activities
  • Low motivation
  • Emotional exhaustion
  • Difficulty concentrating
  • Changes in sleep
  • Changes in appetite
  • Feelings of hopelessness
  • Social withdrawal
  • Difficulty managing everyday responsibilities

If you notice significant or persistent changes in how you feel or function, professional support may be helpful.
