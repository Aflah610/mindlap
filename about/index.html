About Mindlap

Mindlap offers online counselling and therapy in Malayalam, so Malayalees in Kerala and around the world can talk to a qualified psychologist in their own language, from wherever they are.

What we do

We provide confidential online sessions for individuals and couples, for concerns such as anxiety, stress, depression, relationship difficulties, trauma, OCD and ADHD. Sessions take place over video, so you can join from home in Kerala, elsewhere in India or abroad.

Every session is with a psychologist who holds a postgraduate degree in psychology. You can read each psychologist's qualifications, areas of focus and way of working on their profile.

How we work

Our psychologists use evidence-based approaches such as CBT, DBT, ERP, JPMR, ACT, SFBT, EMDR, MBCT, Narrative Therapy, chosen to fit each person's needs and pace. What you share stays private: how we collect, store and protect your information is explained in our privacy policy.

Booking is simple. Choose a psychologist and a time online, or message our coordinator on WhatsApp and we will find a time that suits you.
