Emtesan Dilna is a Consultant Psychologist at Mindlap, offering online counselling and psychological support in Malayalam. She works with individuals experiencing mood disorders, anxiety, ADHD, relationship concerns, child behavioural concerns, OCD, grief and emotional difficulties, and focus and learning difficulties.

Her approach focuses on understanding individual needs and experiences through evidence-informed psychological practices. She aims to create a safe, empathetic, and non-judgmental space where individuals feel comfortable exploring their concerns and working towards positive change.

What Emtesan helps with

Mood Disorders
Supporting individuals in managing mood changes and emotional difficulties.
Anxiety Disorders
Helping individuals understand and manage excessive worry, fear, and anxiety.
ADHD
Supporting individuals with attention, impulsivity, and hyperactivity-related challenges.
Relationship Concerns
Helping individuals navigate relationship difficulties and improve communication.
Child Behavioural Concerns
Supporting children in managing behavioural, emotional, and developmental challenges.
OCD
Helping individuals manage obsessive thoughts and repetitive behaviours.
Grief & Emotional Difficulties
Helping individuals cope with loss, emotional pain, and difficult life experiences.
Focus & Learning Difficulties
Supporting individuals in improving concentration, learning skills, and managing academic challenges.

How Emtesan works

Evidence-Informed Practice
Using scientifically supported psychological approaches to guide care.
Individual-Centred Care
Understanding each individual's unique needs, experiences, and concerns.
