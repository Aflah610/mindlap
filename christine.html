Christine is a consultant psychologist at MindLap, offering online counselling and therapy in Malayalam. Sessions with Christine focus on relationship & marital concerns, anxiety & stress related issues, ptsd & trauma recovery and personality & self-awareness.

Christine works with evidence-based methods including CBT, ACT, DBT, SFBT and EMDR. Sessions are held online over video, so clients across Kerala, from Kozhikode and Malappuram to Kochi and Thiruvananthapuram, and Malayalees living in other parts of India or abroad can attend from home.

What Christine helps with

Relationship & Marital Concerns
Strengthening communication, mutual understanding and meaningful connections among couples.
Anxiety & Stress Related Issues
Developing skills and healthy coping strategies for better management.
PTSD & Trauma Recovery
Promoting healing, emotional stability and healthy coping for trauma-related challenges.
Personality & Self-Awareness
Understanding behaviour patterns and strengthening self-awareness.
Depression Support & Emotional Well-being
Encouraging emotional well-being, hope and resilience.
