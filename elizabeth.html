Elizabeth Sara Joseph is a Consultant Psychologist at Mindlap, offering online counselling and psychological support in Malayalam. She provides person-centred and solution-focused psychological support for individuals, couples, and families.

Her areas of focus include anxiety, depression, stress, trauma, abuse, PTSD, relationship and family concerns, personal growth, self-esteem, life transitions, sleep and lifestyle concerns, grief, and burnout.

She aims to create a safe, compassionate, and non-judgmental space where individuals can explore their concerns, recognise their strengths, and work towards meaningful personal growth and emotional well-being.

What Elizabeth helps with

Anxiety, Depression & Stress
Supporting individuals in managing anxiety, low mood, and stress while building emotional resilience.
Trauma, Abuse & PTSD
Helping individuals process traumatic experiences and work towards emotional recovery.
Relationships & Family Concerns
Supporting individuals and families in navigating relationship challenges, family dynamics, and improving communication.
Personal Growth & Self-Esteem
Helping individuals build self-awareness, confidence, and a positive sense of self.
Life Transitions & Adjustment
Supporting individuals through major life changes and adjustment-related difficulties.
Sleep & Lifestyle Concerns
Addressing sleep difficulties and lifestyle factors that affect mental well-being.
Grief & Emotional Difficulties
Helping individuals cope with loss, grief, and other emotional challenges.
Burnout & Stress Management
Supporting individuals in managing emotional exhaustion and developing healthy coping strategies.

How Elizabeth works

Person-Centred Approach
Providing a safe and non-judgmental space where individuals can express themselves freely and work towards personal growth.
Solution-Focused Approach
Building on individual strengths to identify practical solutions and encourage positive change.
Supportive & Compassionate Care
Offering a warm, respectful environment with empathy and understanding.
Mindfulness & Self-Awareness
Helping individuals stay present and develop greater self-awareness for a balanced life.
