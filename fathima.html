Fathima Sidhyc is a consultant psychologist at Mindlap, offering online counselling and therapy in Malayalam. Sessions with Fathima focus on anxiety, depression, OCD and ADHD.

Fathima works with evidence-based methods including CBT and Narrative Therapy. Sessions are held online over video, so clients across Kerala, from Kozhikode and Malappuram to Kochi and Thiruvananthapuram, and Malayalees living in other parts of India or abroad can attend from home.
