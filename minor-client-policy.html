Minor Client Policy

MindLap: Where Mind Finds Comfort · Last updated: 11 November 2026

1. Introduction

MindLap is committed to ensuring the safety, dignity, privacy and well-being of all individuals accessing its psychological counselling and therapy services. This policy establishes the age eligibility requirements, parental registration procedures and safeguarding standards applicable to minor clients.

2. Age Eligibility

MindLap provides psychological counselling and therapy services exclusively to individuals aged 15 years and above.

  • Individuals below 15 years: MindLap does not provide counselling or therapy services to individuals below 15 years of age.
  • Individuals aged 15 to 17 years: Services are available only when registration is completed by a parent or legally authorised guardian, with the required consent.
  • Individuals aged 18 years and above: Individuals may independently register for and access services.

MindLap reserves the right to verify the age of any client before confirming a consultation.

3. Parental Registration and Consent

For clients aged 15 to 17 years, registration must be completed by a parent or legally authorised guardian.

The parent or guardian must:

  • Provide accurate personal and contact information.
  • Provide the minor's correct date of birth and relevant details.
  • Establish their relationship or legal authority as guardian.
  • Provide explicit consent for the minor to access psychological counselling and therapy services.
  • Acknowledge and agree to the applicable service terms, policies and consultation procedures.

MindLap will not initiate therapy services for minor clients until the required parental or guardian registration and consent have been completed and verified.

4. Confidentiality and Privacy

MindLap respects the privacy and confidentiality of all minor clients. Personal information and details shared during therapy sessions will be handled in accordance with applicable data protection laws and professional ethical standards.

Confidentiality may be limited in circumstances involving:

  • Serious or imminent risk of harm to the minor or another individual.
  • Suspected or disclosed abuse, neglect, exploitation or safeguarding concerns.
  • Legal obligations or directions from competent authorities.
  • Situations requiring intervention to protect the safety and well-being of the minor.

Parental registration does not provide unrestricted access to the minor's confidential therapy discussions. Any necessary information-sharing will be guided by applicable laws, professional obligations and safeguarding requirements.

5. Child Protection and Safeguarding

MindLap is committed to protecting minor clients from abuse, exploitation, harassment and other forms of harm.

Any disclosure or identification of concerns relating to physical abuse, sexual abuse, emotional abuse, neglect, exploitation, online grooming or serious safety risks will be addressed through appropriate safeguarding procedures.

Where applicable, MindLap will comply with mandatory reporting obligations under relevant Indian legislation, including the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012.

Appropriate authorities or emergency support services may be contacted when necessary to protect the minor.

6. Parental and Guardian Responsibilities

Parents and legally authorised guardians are responsible for completing the registration and consent requirements and providing accurate information relevant to the minor's care.

They are expected to:

  • Support the minor's participation in the therapeutic process.
  • Cooperate with reasonable safeguarding requirements.
  • Provide relevant information when necessary for the minor's well-being.
  • Respect the professional boundaries and confidentiality requirements of therapy.

MindLap may recommend parental or guardian involvement when necessary for the minor's safety and well-being, subject to applicable legal and professional requirements.

7. Online Consultation and Communication

All online therapy sessions are conducted through MindLap-approved platforms, including Google Meet and Zoom.

To maintain client privacy and professional boundaries:

  • Direct personal phone calls and WhatsApp calls between therapists and minor clients are not permitted.
  • Session-related communication must take place through officially approved MindLap communication channels.
  • Parents or guardians will be informed about relevant registration and scheduling procedures.
  • Therapists are required to maintain appropriate professional boundaries throughout the therapeutic relationship.

8. Reporting Concerns

Minor clients, parents and legally authorised guardians may contact MindLap to report concerns relating to safety, inappropriate conduct, confidentiality or any safeguarding issue associated with the services provided. Write to support@mindlap.in or call +91 75940 00774.

All reported concerns will be reviewed and addressed through appropriate organisational procedures, in accordance with applicable laws and professional standards.

9. Policy Amendments

MindLap reserves the right to amend or update this policy whenever necessary to reflect changes in applicable legislation, professional standards or organisational requirements. Any updated version will be published on the official website.

By registering a minor client, the parent or legally authorised guardian acknowledges the requirements outlined in this policy and agrees to comply with the applicable registration, consent and safeguarding procedures of MindLap.
