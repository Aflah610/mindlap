Theresa P Thomas is a consultant psychologist at MindLap, offering online counselling and therapy in Malayalam. Sessions with Theresa focus on stress & anxiety management, behavioral change and anger & emotional regulation.

Theresa works with evidence-based methods including CBT, DBT and ACT. Sessions are held online over video, so clients across Kerala, from Kozhikode and Malappuram to Kochi and Thiruvananthapuram, and Malayalees living in other parts of India or abroad can attend from home.
