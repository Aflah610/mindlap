Rashin PK is a consultant psychologist at Mindlap, offering online counselling and therapy in Malayalam. Sessions with Rashin focus on mood disorders, personality disorders, couples therapy and emotional impulsiveness & decision-making.

Rashin works with evidence-based methods including CBT, DBT, ERP and JPMR. Sessions are held online over video, so clients across Kerala, from Kozhikode and Malappuram to Kochi and Thiruvananthapuram, and Malayalees living in other parts of India or abroad can attend from home.

What Rashin helps with

Mood Disorders
Supporting individuals in managing mood-related conditions to improve emotional stability and daily functioning.
Personality Disorders
Helping clients understand long-standing patterns and develop healthier ways of thinking, feeling, and relating.
Couples Therapy
Guiding couples to strengthen communication, resolve conflicts, and build healthier relationships.
Emotional Impulsiveness & Decision-Making
Helping clients regulate impulsive reactions and make thoughtful, value-based decisions.

How Rashin works

Evidence-Based Practice
Using scientifically supported therapeutic approaches to provide effective, high-quality care.
Holistic & Integrative
Addressing emotional, psychological, and lifestyle factors through a comprehensive approach.
Ethical & Confidential
