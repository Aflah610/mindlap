Ashlin is a Consultant Psychologist at Mindlap, offering online counselling and psychological support in Malayalam. She works with individuals experiencing self-esteem concerns, life transitions, burnout, stress and anxiety, depression, and academic difficulties.

Her approach focuses on providing evidence-based, person-centred psychological support tailored to each individual's unique needs and experiences. She aims to create a safe, empathetic, and supportive space where individuals feel heard, understood, and comfortable working towards personal growth and emotional well-being.

What Ashlin helps with

Self-Esteem & Personal Growth
Helping individuals build self-confidence, recognise their strengths, and develop a positive self-image.
Life Transitions & Adjustment
Supporting individuals in navigating life changes and adapting to new situations with confidence.
Burnout
Helping individuals manage chronic stress, emotional exhaustion, and restore personal well-being.
Stress & Anxiety
Helping individuals manage stress, reduce anxiety, and develop healthier coping strategies.
Depression
Supporting individuals in managing depressive symptoms and improving emotional well-being.
Academic Concerns
Supporting individuals in managing academic stress, improving focus, and overcoming learning challenges.

How Ashlin works

Evidence-Based Approach
Using scientifically supported methods to provide effective and reliable psychological support.
Empathetic & Supportive Approach
Creating a safe, non-judgmental space where individuals feel heard, understood, and supported.
Person-Centred Approach
Tailoring psychological support to each individual's unique needs, experiences, and personal goals.
Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT)
