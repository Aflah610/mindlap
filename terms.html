Terms & Conditions

Effective date: 11 November 2026 · Last updated: 11 November 2026

1. Introduction

These Terms and Conditions govern the use of MindLap's website and online psychological consultation services. By registering, booking an appointment, making a payment, or using our services, clients agree to these terms and the applicable MindLap policies.

2. Services and Eligibility

MindLap provides online psychological consultations, counselling, therapeutic support, psychological assessments where appropriate, and follow-up services through qualified mental health professionals.

Clients must provide accurate personal, contact, and relevant consultation-related information. Additional consent and safeguarding requirements apply to services involving minors; see our Minor Client Policy.

3. Appointment Booking and Payment

  • Appointments are confirmed through the Department of Consultation Coordination (CCD).
  • Consultation fees must be paid through authorised payment methods.
  • Clients must retain payment receipts for verification.
  • Duplicate payments and transaction discrepancies will be reviewed by the Accounts Department.
  • Applicable fee changes will be communicated in advance.

4. Consultation Duration and Platforms

Consultations are conducted through Google Meet or Zoom.

  • First session: 60 minutes.
  • Second session onwards: 45 minutes.

The consultation link will be shared approximately three hours before the scheduled session through the client's registered or official communication number.

Clients must ensure stable internet connectivity and functional audio. Camera use is optional, but audio communication must remain available.

5. Communication and Professional Boundaries

All session-related communication and coordination must take place through CCD using official WhatsApp, telephone, or email channels.

Therapists' personal contact numbers will not be shared. Clients must not contact therapists through their private numbers or accounts. Consultations will take place only through designated Google Meet or Zoom links.

6. Cancellation, Rescheduling and Refunds

  • Rescheduling requests must ordinarily be submitted at least 12 hours before the session.
  • One standard rescheduling is permitted per paid appointment.
  • Requests made less than 12 hours before the session will incur a 20% deduction, with the remaining 80% adjusted towards the replacement session.
  • Failure to join within the 15-minute grace period will be treated as a no-show, resulting in forfeiture of the full consultation fee.
  • Consultation fees and unused packages are generally non-refundable and non-transferable.
  • Emergency exceptions may be considered by CCD on a case-by-case basis.

Verified duplicate payments and payment errors will be handled separately by the Accounts Department.

Please refer to the separate Cancellation, Rescheduling and Refund Policy for complete details.

7. Late Arrival and No-Show

A 15-minute grace period is provided. Clients joining within this period may attend the remaining session time, but the consultation will end at its originally scheduled time.

Failure to join within the grace period will result in cancellation of the appointment and forfeiture of the full consultation fee. Fresh payment will be required for another appointment.

8. Therapist Unavailability and Technical Issues

If a therapist becomes unavailable, clients may reschedule or request another suitable therapist, subject to availability, without an additional administrative fee.

Technical interruptions will be assessed by the therapist and CCD. Depending on the circumstances, the session may continue within the remaining time or be rescheduled without an additional consultation fee.

9. Client Responsibilities and Conduct

Clients must provide accurate information, attend sessions on time, maintain a private consultation environment, and communicate respectfully.

Abusive language, harassment, threats, discrimination, or disruptive behaviour are prohibited. Therapists may terminate sessions where safety or professional boundaries are compromised. Incidents will be reported to CCD and escalated to the Project Manager for review and appropriate action.

10. Privacy, Confidentiality and Records

MindLap protects client information in accordance with its Privacy Policy and Client Consent.

Consultation records and personal information will be accessible only to authorised personnel for legitimate professional, administrative, operational, or legal purposes.

Clients may request access to relevant records, subject to applicable legal and professional requirements. Appropriately anonymised information may be used for research, training, and service improvement with suitable safeguards.

11. Recording and Testimonials

Clients must not record, photograph, screenshot, or distribute consultation sessions without appropriate prior consent.

MindLap may use client feedback or testimonials for communication and promotional purposes only with appropriate privacy safeguards and consent where required.

12. Emergency Situations and Professional Limitations

MindLap's online services are not a substitute for emergency psychiatric care or immediate crisis intervention. Clients facing an immediate risk of harm must contact the nearest hospital, emergency service, or appropriate local authority.

Therapeutic outcomes cannot be guaranteed, and online consultations may not be suitable for every situation.

Website content, logos, designs, text, and educational materials are owned by or licensed to MindLap. Unauthorised copying, reproduction, or distribution is prohibited.

14. Complaints and Discontinuation of Services

Clients may submit complaints through official communication channels. The Consultation Coordinator will record and forward complaints to the Project Manager for review and appropriate action.

MindLap may restrict, suspend, or discontinue services in cases of policy violations, fraudulent information, inappropriate conduct, or professional and safety concerns, subject to applicable law.
